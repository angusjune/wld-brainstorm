# Rendered design review

Review the actual screenshots before showing a solution set. Use the user's intent and the active profile as the standard. If a relevant reference screenshot was supplied, compare it alongside the output; HTML and token compliance alone do not establish visual quality.

1. **Set:** Hide the captions mentally. Can you identify each direction by its composition or interaction? Explain the useful choice each offers. If two are interchangeable, revise the weaker one while keeping the strong direction intact. Pure visual exploration may keep the same interaction and markup.
2. **Screen:** Identify the first focal point and the next reading step. Check grouping, alignment, distribution of visual weight, and the rhythm between dense and quiet areas. Related content should feel related without boxing every item. Judge whitespace by its role in the composition, not by filling the viewport.
3. **Details:** Inspect important controls and information groups at readable scale. Check type proportions and line breaks, number/unit baselines, icon weight and optical alignment, padding, surface edges, and consistency between repeated elements. Preserve legibility, tap targets, and clear selected/disabled states. Capture closer browser screenshots when the gallery is too small to judge.

Record at most three high-impact findings as **location → visible problem → intended improvement**. For example: “The summary label, amount, and action have equal emphasis; make the amount the focal point and group its label more closely.” Give the revision worker room to solve the problem. Fix composition before fine spacing. Keep deliberate differences between directions instead of polishing all three into the same screen.

Compare the revised screenshots with the first render. Record which findings improved and any remaining tradeoff in the stage's `design-review.md`. Mechanical validation determines whether the output works; this review explains its design quality. Use concrete observations rather than invented numerical pass thresholds.
